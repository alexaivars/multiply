Add to iPhone or iPad
  1. Open this app’s secure web address in Safari.
  2. Open Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on. Tap Add.
  4. Open Multiply from your Home Screen while connected to download the app for offline use.
